浮想 · 隐私政策

我们收集什么

什么都不收集。「浮想」没有账号、没有广告、没有统计分析工具，也不会把任何数据发给开发者或第三方。

Nothing. Fuxiang has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it never sends any data to the developer or to anyone else.

你的灵感存在哪里

你记下的文字、图片、类别和设置，只保存在你自己的 iPhone 上。删除 App 会同时删除这些内容。

Everything you write, the images you add, your categories and settings are stored only on your own iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them too.

网页版

网页版（添加到主屏幕的「浮想」）同样什么都不上传：内容只存在这台设备的浏览器里。从主屏幕删掉它，或清除 Safari 的网站数据，会同时删除这些内容。

The web version (Fuxiang added to your Home Screen) uploads nothing either: everything stays in this device's browser. Removing it from the Home Screen or clearing Safari's website data deletes it.

相机和相册

当你给灵感添加图片时，App 会请求使用相机或相册。选中的图片只存进这条灵感里，不会上传到任何地方。

When you add a picture to an idea, the app asks for access to the camera or photo library. The chosen picture is saved inside that idea on your device and is never uploaded.

备份

「更多 → 导出备份」会生成一个文件，由你决定把它存到哪里（例如"文件"App 或 iCloud 云盘）。这个文件只在你自己选择的地方。

"More → Export backup" creates a file and you choose where to keep it (for example the Files app or iCloud Drive). It only goes where you send it.

儿童

「浮想」不收集任何人的信息，包括儿童。

Fuxiang does not collect information from anyone, including children.

联系

有问题可以通过支持页面联系我。

Questions? Reach me through the support page.
